Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Ekagro collects, why, and how it's handled. Ekagro is a task management application; this policy covers the account and productivity data you provide while using it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password. Your password is never stored in plain text \u2014 it is hashed with bcrypt before being saved.
  • Productivity data: the tasks, notes, habits, bills, and projects you create, along with their associated details (due dates, categories, tags, etc.).
  • Login security data: temporary one-time codes (OTPs) used for password resets and optional two-factor authentication, which expire automatically and are deleted after use.

What we do not collect

  • Ekagro does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.
  • Ekagro does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services used

  • MongoDB Atlas \u2014 stores your account and productivity data securely.
  • Google Gemini API \u2014 if you use the AI Insights feature, a summary of your task data for the selected period is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a natural-language productivity summary. This happens only when you explicitly request it.
  • Email delivery (SMTP) \u2014 used only to send password-reset codes and optional two-factor authentication codes to your registered email address.

How your data is protected

  • Passwords are hashed, never stored or transmitted in plain text.
  • Sessions use signed JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) rather than persistent tracking cookies.
  • All data is scoped to your account; no other user can access your tasks, notes, or personal information.
  • Requests are protected against common web vulnerabilities (injection attacks, unauthorized field tampering, and brute-force login attempts).

Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Account Settings. Deleted accounts enter a 7-day recovery window (in case of accidental deletion), after which all associated data is permanently and irreversibly removed.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct inaccurate information, or request permanent deletion at any time by contacting us using the details below.
